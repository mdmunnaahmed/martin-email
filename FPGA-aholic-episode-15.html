First Lead-Free Flip-Chip FPGAs Achieve Highest Space Component Certification

Microchip's RTG4 FPGAs have reached a groundbreaking achievement by becoming the first lead-free flip-chip FPGAs to earn QML Class V qualification, the highest certification level for space components by the Defense Logistics Agency (DLA). This milestone is critical for ensuring mission assurance on deep space, human-rated, and national security missions. With over 150,000 logic elements, low power consumption, and immunity to configuration upsets, RTG4 FPGAs are designed for high-performance space applications.
